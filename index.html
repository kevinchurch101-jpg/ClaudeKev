Course Intel — Fazio Canyons at Omni Barton Creek

A first Tour visit. Tom Fazio's Canyons course opened in 1999 and came back from a $5 million restoration in October 2022 that added more than 400 yards of back tees (Haslbauer). It plays as a par 72 at 7,451 yards: four par 3s, ten par 4s, four par 5s, bermuda throughout. Bamford's course guide has fairways averaging 38 yards wide (25 to 55), 42 bunkers, greens of about 5,300 square feet, and a driveable par 4 of 344 yards. The par 3s are the long part: three play over 215 yards. The writers count the par 4s differently: Sharpside has six of the ten at 448 yards or more, Bamford has six under 450 and none over 500. All four par 5s are reachable (Coley, Haslbauer). Elevation changes on nearly every hole, with forced carries and ravines on the back nine. Writers disagree on how much water is in play (six holes per Rawlings, ten per Bamford).

It should be soft. Bamford logs 83 mm of rain in October and 67 mm so far in November. Sobel expects 20-under or better to win; Haslbauer expects something short of the fall's birdie-fests because misses are punished more here.
